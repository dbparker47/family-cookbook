    Section 1.7 Three-grain Pancakes

    Ingredients:
    🔗
    \(1 \frac{3}{4}\) cup whole-wheat flour
    \(\frac{1}{3}\) cup cornmeal
    \(\frac{1}{3}\) cup rolled oats
    2 teaspoons baking powder
    \(\frac{1}{2}\) teaspoon salt
    \(\frac{1}{2}\) teaspoon baking soda
    \(\frac{1}{2}\) teaspoon cinnamon
    \(\frac{1}{8}\) teaspoon nutmeg
    \(1 \frac{3}{4}\) cup milk
    4 tablespoons butter, melted
    \(\frac{1}{4}\) cup honey
    3 extra-large eggs
    1 teaspoon vanilla extract
    Chocolate chips, bananas, blueberries, or other creative mix-ins (optional)
    Instructions
    1. Mix dry ingredients together.
      🔗
      🔗
    2. Mix wet ingredients together.
      🔗
      🔗
    3. Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients. Mix until just combined.
      🔗
      🔗
    4. Cook on the griddle with mix-ins.
      🔗
      🔗
    🔗
    Notes:
    • I have made these with blueberries, sliced bananas, and chocolate chips. I add the mix-ins to the batter right after I put it on the griddle and cover it with batter so it doesn’t burn when I flip the pancakes.
      🔗
      🔗
